    How to play

    1. Each player has four pawns in a coloured yard. Get all four round the board and into the centre to finish.
    2. Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out onto your start square. A 6 also earns another roll.
    3. Pawns travel clockwise, 51 squares round the track, then up your own coloured column to home. You need the exact number to finish.
    4. Land on a lone or stacked rival pawn to send it back to its yard. Capturing earns another roll.
    5. Starred squares and every start square are safe: no captures there.
    6. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
    7. Tap the die or the Roll button, then tap a glowing pawn or its marker. Drag to turn the board, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to reset.
